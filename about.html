01 / MY STORY

A CREATOR AT HEART.

I enjoy the process of taking an idea from a simple thought to something real. For me, development is where imagination meets problem-solving: experimenting, building, testing, and discovering what makes an experience feel right.

Don Yovanny is a place to showcase the games I've created and the different interactive experiences I hope to bring to life in the future. Each project is an opportunity to learn something new and create a world with its own personality.

02 / WHERE THE JOURNEY BEGINS

MEET NIKO.

Niko's Great Adventure is my first featured game project: a colorful browser-based adventure designed for young explorers ages 3–9.

The idea is to make discovery feel inviting through playful challenges, spoken instructions, and opportunities to explore at a comfortable pace. I'm especially interested in thoughtful experiences that consider different learning styles and abilities.
